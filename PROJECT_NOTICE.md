BlogPost is a personal indie project by Bogus. Please read this before you rely on it, open an issue, or send a request.

## In short

- **One maintainer, limited access:** BlogPost is run by one solo indie maker who is often away from his devices for long periods.
- **Slow or no replies:** issues, requests, complaints, legal notices, security reports, takedown requests and other urgent contacts may not be read, answered or acted on promptly. The delay can be long.
- **May pause or disappear:** the repository and the site may be paused, suspended or removed, including when the maintainer regains access and reviews it.
- **Opinions, not endorsements:** posts are personal opinion and experience. AI tool mentions are not endorsements and are not sponsored.
- **Free, as is:** the skills are free and provided as is, without warranty (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

## Maintainer availability and urgent reports

- **Limited access:** the maintainer often has no access to devices, the project's accounts or its contact channels for long periods. Access returns only when he is back.
- **Urgent reports wait:** during that time, a complaint, legal notice, security report, takedown request, platform report or any other urgent contact may wait until he is back. If something on this site affects you, open an issue anyway; it will be seen when he returns.
- **No guarantees:** there is no guaranteed response time and no guarantee that the site stays online.

## Requests and the BlogPostIdeas bot

- Requests are made through the [Request form](https://github.com/klanarongz2009-sketch/BlogPost/issues/new?template=request.yml) on GitHub Issues.
- A bot named **BlogPostIdeas** may answer requests. It is an AI that runs on Wabi, an AI assistant platform. It is not the maintainer.
- The bot passes requests to the maintainer's own AI assistant, which logs them for him. Neither the bot nor the assistant can promise that the maintainer will act on a request, or when.
- The bot does not receive or handle legal notices, security reports or takedown requests, and it cannot make decisions for the maintainer.
- Requests are public. Do not include personal information, passwords or tokens.

## Why the project may pause or close

- **Maintainer away:** updates, replies and reviews stop while the maintainer has no access.
- **Closed without a stated reason:** the project may close without an explanation.
- **A claim about content:** if there is a claim about any content or link here, the affected part, or the whole site, may be taken down.
- **A platform change:** a free service the site or the bot depends on (GitHub Pages, Wabi, a linked platform) may change, limit or stop.
