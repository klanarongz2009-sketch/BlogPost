# About

I go by **Bogus**. I am an indie maker: no company, no team, no budget.

## What I do

I call myself an **AI operator** and **digital architect**. In practice that means:

- I write the specification: what the thing should do, the rules it must follow, and what counts as done.
- I coordinate AI agents that do much of the implementation: code, documents, releases, checks.
- I review and test the results, and send work back when it is wrong.

I do not claim to have trained a foundation model. My projects use existing models, mostly free, local, or open-weight ones, and wrap them in rules, tools, and tests.

## Why "indie for indie"

Most AI guides assume a paid plan, a team, or a big machine. I have none of those. What works for me is free tiers that reset, local and open-weight models, and agents that keep working while I am away. I write it down here so other indie makers can reuse what works and skip what did not.

## How I like to work

These are my own working rules, not advice for everyone:

- **Evidence over claims.** An AI saying "done" or "not found" is not proof. I want the file, the test result, or the page checked.
- **Separate facts, guesses, and unknowns.** Every report should say which is which.
- **Free first.** I avoid paid services and billing. If a tool costs real money, I say so.
- **Let the agent finish.** I would rather the agent carry the work through than hand me instructions to do it myself.
- **Privacy.** Public work does not carry personal information.

## Availability

I am often away from my devices for long periods. Issues, messages, and reports may wait a long time, and this site may pause. See the [project notice](https://github.com/klanarongz2009-sketch/BlogPost/blob/main/PROJECT_NOTICE.md).
