# AI tools I use

These are tools I actually use, and what I use each one for. This is my experience, not a review and not an endorsement. Nothing here is sponsored, and I get nothing if you sign up. Check each tool's own pricing and terms before you rely on it; free tiers and limits change.

## Assistants

- **[Sesame](https://www.sesame.com/)**: my main assistant. I use it to run sub-agents through custom skills, schedules, a Linux VM, a browser, and connected apps (email, calendar, GitHub through the VM). Most of the work on this site and on TER-AI was done through it.
- **[Wabi](https://wabi.ai/)**: my backup assistant for TER-AI work, not a replacement. It has separate main, memory, and dream-journal agents. I have used it for repository work such as dependency alert fixes.
- **Muze**: another assistant I use. Its memory holds up to 100 entries, so I import a trimmed portable memory snapshot into it.
- **[Zapia](https://www.zapia.com/)**: I use it for chat and for testing how well an AI follows a verbatim text-return prompt.
- **[Overlay.one](https://overlay.one/)**: scored a full exact match in my verbatim text-return test.

## Models

- **[Qwen](https://qwen.ai/)** (chat at [chat.qwen.ai](https://chat.qwen.ai/)): scored a full exact match in my verbatim text-return test. Small open-weight Qwen models also appear in my own prototypes.
- **Local and open-weight models**: my TER-AI chat model (TERAI1, beta) uses a local Gemma model to write replies, with TER-AI rules exposed as tools. For an indie maker, local models mean no per-call cost and no service shutting you off.

## Building platforms

I give app ideas to different AIs and let each one build a new app from scratch. See [MiniApps](../miniapps/) for each app and the AI that built it.

## How I choose (opinion)

- Free or free-tier first, and I say clearly when something costs money.
- I test the specific thing I need (for me, often Thai text) instead of trusting benchmark claims.
- I keep a backup assistant, because any service can hit a limit or go down on the day you need it.
