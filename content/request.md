# Request something

Want a post on a topic, a new free skill, a MiniApp idea, or a fix? Open a request:

**[Open a request on GitHub](https://github.com/klanarongz2009-sketch/BlogPost/issues/new?template=request.yml)**

What happens next:

- A bot named **BlogPostIdeas** may reply first. It is an AI, not Bogus. It runs on Wabi, an AI assistant platform.
- The request is passed to Bogus's own AI assistant, which logs it and tells him.
- Bogus may take a long time to see it, and may not act on it. There is no promised response time. See the [project notice](https://github.com/klanarongz2009-sketch/BlogPost/blob/main/PROJECT_NOTICE.md).

Please do not put personal information, passwords, or anything private in a request. Requests are public.
