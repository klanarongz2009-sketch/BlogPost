Indie for indie

I am Bogus, a solo indie maker. I design AI systems and workflows, coordinate AI agents, and review and test what they produce. The AI does much of the implementation. This blog is for other indie developers and creators who work alone or in very small teams and want to do more with AI without paying for much.

What you will find here:

  • About: who I am as a maker, and how I work.
  • How I use AI: sub-agents, memory, verification, scheduled routines, and Thai AI quality testing, with real examples.
  • AI tools I use: the assistants and models I actually use, and what I use each one for. Opinions, not endorsements.
  • Free skill library: thirteen standalone SKILL.md files that work with any AI assistant or agent. MIT licensed.
  • MiniApps: new apps built from scratch by different AIs, one AI per app.
  • Projects: TER-AI, Tagentic1, and other projects.
  • Thai version of the key pages.
