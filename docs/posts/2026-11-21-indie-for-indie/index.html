Indie for indie: why this blog exists

2026-11-21

I build alone. No company, no team, no budget. For a long time that meant small projects. With AI agents, it now means a Thai AI toolkit with releases and tests, a Telegram bot, a community on GitHub Discussions, games on several platforms, and AI music released under my artist name.

The agents do much of the implementation. My job is the part they are bad at: deciding what to build, writing the rules, and refusing to accept "done" until it is checked.

This blog is indie for indie. Everything here is aimed at people in the same position:

  • Free or near-free tools. I avoid paid services. When something costs money, I will say so.
  • Solo workflows. How one person runs many agents without losing track.
  • Things you can take. The skill library is MIT licensed. Copy it, change it, ship it.
  • Honest results. Opinions are marked as opinions. Test results come with their limits.

If you are an indie maker, start with How I use AI and pick one skill to try.
